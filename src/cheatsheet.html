On your turn

Extra Attack only works with the Attack action; you can't swing twice and cast a leveled spell in the same turn.

    Combos and gotchas

    • Mind Sliver, then a save spell next turn (Command, Grease, Shatter): the −1d4 lasts until the end of your next turn. Also sets up Virion's and Zephyra's saves.
    • Faerie Fire before the party's attack round gives everyone advantage.
    • Bonus action spell rule: cast Misty Step or Sanctuary and your only other spell that turn can be a 1-action cantrip.
    • Concentration: Caustic Brew, Faerie Fire and Detect Magic can't run together. Damage forces a CON save (+5), DC 10 or half the damage.
    • Light crossbow has Loading: only one shot per turn even with Extra Attack. Use the launcher instead.
    • Ritual Caster spells (Detect Magic, Identify) are ritual-only (+10 min) unless you also prepare them as artificer spells.
    • Prepared: you can prepare 7 (INT +5, half level 2); the sheet shows 6, so one slot is open (Alarm?).

    Tracker

    Tap a pip to mark it used. Long rest also restores HP and clears temp HP. It doesn't refill Mind Sharpener: it regains 1d4 charges at dawn, so tap those back yourself. Saved on this device only.

    Equipment

    Healing potions and consumables show up as Use an item in the simulator's action step; ending the turn uses one up. Long rest doesn't touch equipment. Saved on this device only.
